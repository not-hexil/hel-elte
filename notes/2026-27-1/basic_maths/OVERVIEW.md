# Basic Mathematics

## Requirements, Exam & Grading

### Passing Requirements
To pass the course, you must obtain a **practice grade of at least 2** by satisfying both of the following conditions:
1. **Attendance:** Compulsory; you may miss **at most 6 classes** during the semester. Missing more than 6 classes results in no grade and disqualifies you from both the retake test and the re-examination.
2. **Test Performance:** Achieve a practice grade of at least 2 based on your Great Tests and Short Tests.

### Preliminary Practice Grade Calculation
* **Case 1 — Passed all 3 Great Tests:** Your score is the sum of your three Great Tests plus Short Test reward points (up to 10 points). *(Note: If your Short Test total is 0–9 points, your preliminary grade is reduced by one grade value.)*
* **Case 2 — Passed 2 Great Tests, failed 1:** Preliminary grade is `1` (not yet registered in NEPTUN), and you are eligible for the **Retake Test**.
* **Case 3 — Failed 2 or more Great Tests:** Final practice grade is `1 (fail)` and is registered in NEPTUN immediately; you must take the **Practice Grade Re-Examination**.

### Grade Boundaries
| Total Points | Grade |
| :--- | :--- |
| **0 – 50** | `1` (Fail) |
| **51 – 68** | `2` (Pass) |
| **69 – 91** | `3` (Average) |
| **92 – 120** | `4` (Commendable) |
| **121 – 150** | `5` (Excellent) |

### Retake Test & Re-Examination
* **Retake Test:** Available if you failed at most one Great Test. If you failed one test, you retake that test; if you passed all three, you may retake the one with your lowest score to improve your grade. A higher score overrides the original score, and the recalculated grade becomes final in NEPTUN (missing the retake locks in your preliminary grade).
* **Practice Grade Re-Examination:** Held during the examination period for students with a final practice grade of `1` in NEPTUN. All prior semester scores are cleared. Only grades `1` (`0–29` points) or `2` (`30–75` points) can be earned.

## Test Structure

### 1. Short Tests (10 Quizzes)
* **Schedule & Format:** Weekly starting from Week 2; 6-minute Canvas quiz with 2 questions covering the previous week's two classes.
* **Rules:** Taken in person in the classroom on your own device (laptop, phone, etc.). Missed quizzes score `0` regardless of the reason, with no retakes.
* **Scoring:** `2 points` per quiz (`20 points` max).
  * `0–9 points`: Unsuccessful (deducts 1 full grade from your preliminary practice grade).
  * `10–20 points`: Successful; any points **above 10** (up to 10 points) are added as reward points to your Great Test total.

### 2. Great Tests (3 Midterm/Semester Tests)
* **Format:** Written tests, **90 minutes** each, **50 points** maximum per test.
* **Pass Threshold:** `17–50 points` (pass); `0–16 points` (fail).
* **Absences:** Unauthorized absences score `0` with no resit. Only one authorized emergency absence (reported *before* the test with valid documentation, such as a doctor's note) may be resat at a later arranged date.

### 3. Re-Examination Test
* **Format:** **120 minutes**, **75 points** total.
* **Structure:** `3 + 3 + 3 = 9` questions (3 questions from each of the three Great Test topic blocks).
* **Minimum Requirement:** You must score at least **7 points in each of the three parts** separately (`7-7-7`), plus at least `30 points` overall, to pass.

## Topics Covered

The course reviews secondary-level mathematics to fill foundational gaps while introducing university-level concepts across three main areas:
* **Precalculus** (Modules 1–9)
* **Linear Algebra** (Modules 10–21)
* **Functions** (Modules 22–24)
