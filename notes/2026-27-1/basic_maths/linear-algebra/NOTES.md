# Linear Algebra Subject Summary

Course notes and theorem index.

## Note Files & Topics

- **[00 - Important Exam Concepts](00-important-exam-concepts.md)**
  High-yield oral and written exam reference covering all chapter Control Questions, key definitions, and full required proofs vs. statement-only theorems.

- **[01 - Matrix & Determinants](01-matrix-determinants.md)**
  Matrix operations over $\mathbb{K} \in \{\mathbb{R}, \mathbb{C}\}$, special matrices, transpose and Hermitian adjoint ($A^*$), block matrices, minor matrices, cofactor expansion, determinant properties, and the adjugate inverse formula.

- **[02 - Vector Spaces & Spans](02-vector-spaces-span.md)**
  The 10 vector space axioms, $\mathbb{K}^n$, matrix-vector products, the 2-step subspace test, linear combinations, $\text{Span}(x_1, \dots, x_k)$, standard unit vectors, and finite vs. infinite dimensionality.

- **[03 - Independence, Basis & Dimension](03-independence-basis-dim.md)**
  Dependence equations, Theorem of Unique Representation, diminution and extension of vector systems, coordinates, existence of a basis, the Exchange Theorem, dimension invariance, and the "4 small statements."

- **[04 - Rank, Systems & Inverses](04-rank-systems-inverses.md)**
  Row/column space equality ($\text{rank}(A)$), homogeneous systems and $\text{Ker}(A)$, affine solution set structure, Rank-Nullity, Gauss-Jordan elimination (pivots, reduced tables, prohibited rows), 5 characterizations of regular/singular matrices, and $[A \mid I]$ inversion.

- **[05 - Eigenvalues & Diagonalization](05-eigenvalues-diagonalization.md)**
  Linear transformations in $\mathbb{K}^n$, characteristic polynomial $P_A(\lambda) = \det(A - \lambda I)$, spectrum over $\mathbb{R}$ vs. $\mathbb{C}$, algebraic vs. geometric multiplicity ($1 \le g(\lambda) \le a(\lambda) \le n$), Eigenvector Basis (E.B.), similarity invariance, and the 4-step diagonalization algorithm ($D = C^{-1}AC$).

- **[06 - Euclidean Spaces](06-euclidean-spaces.md)**
  Inner product axioms, Euclidean norm, normalization, orthogonality to vectors and subspaces, O.S./O.N.S./O.B./O.N.B., Pythagorean theorem, Gaussian normal equations, Gram matrix, Fourier expansion, Projection Theorem ($x = P(x) + Q(x)$), Gram-Schmidt orthogonalization, Cauchy's inequality, Triangle inequality, and $k$-parallelepiped volumes.
